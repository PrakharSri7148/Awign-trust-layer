`china.jpg` and `flower.jpg` are the two sample photographs shipped with
scikit-learn (`sklearn.datasets.load_sample_images`; see its images/README.txt for
the Creative Commons attribution). Real-world photographs used by the check tests.
They are 640x427, so the resolution check correctly rejects them.

`astronaut.png` is NASA's official portrait of astronaut Eileen Collins (public
domain), as bundled with scikit-image (`skimage.data.astronaut`). A real photograph
with a clear frontal face, 512x512, used by the face-check tests.
