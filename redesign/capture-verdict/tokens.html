New: FraudGauge (cap-gauge)

60 segments (same cell language as the quality meters), clockwise from 12 o’clock; the 50% threshold mark sits at 6 o’clock; label, count-up value and caption are centred. Colour is --tone (set by cap-gauge--ok / --fail); the word VERIFIED / FLAGGED FOR REVIEW always sits next to it. Size: --cap-gauge (188 px desktop, 168 tablet, 132 phone). The element is role="meter" with aria-valuetext="43.2%, threshold 50%".

New: status badge (cap-badge) and celebration (cap-burst)

Left to right: accepted, accepted with the celebration frozen mid-burst, flagged (no celebration). --b is the tone, --c, --dx, --dy are set per dot. Reduced motion (OS or html[data-motion="reduce"]) hides the dots.

New: submission id + copy (cap-idrow)

Why? card: reason, fusion rows (4 tones) and SHAP bars

New: fetched rows (cap-frow): received, not available, problem
